Who We Are

Kouryaku Lab is an independent, fan-run guide site. We focus on mobile games that are popular in Japan but have never received an official global release or English localization — games where players outside Japan have little to no reliable English-language information to work from.

All guides on this site are unofficial and fan-made, based on publicly available information. Kouryaku Lab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the developers or publishers of the games covered here.
