Your First Ship: Shuttle, Star Barge or Sparrow

The three ships you can buy at the start, compared with the game's own numbers — and which fits which plan.

01

Conclusion

In the default start you begin on New Boston with 480,000 credits and a mortgage, and the local shipyard sells three ships: the Shuttle, the Star Barge and the Sparrow.

Combining the ship data with the job payment formula, the Shuttle stands out for making money early: it's the cheapest and has 6 bunks, and each passenger pays like 10 tons of cargo. The Star Barge carries the most cargo (50 tons), and the Sparrow is the fighter of the three.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Endless Sky's ship and start data and its ship-stat code (no first-hand play experience). It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

04

First Steps

  1. 1Pick a ship based on the plan above. If you're unsure, the data favors the Shuttle for steady income.
  2. 2Take jobs from the job board that go the same direction — passengers if you have spare bunks.
  3. 3Fill spare cargo space with a commodity that sells higher at your destination. See Making Money.
  4. 4Save up for a bigger ship rather than many small outfits; see Ships for prices and where each is sold.
05

FAQ

Can I change ships later?

Yes — sell or keep your first ship and buy another at any shipyard that sells it.

Why does the Shuttle earn so much?

Passenger jobs pay 150 × (jumps + 1) × 10 per passenger plus 2,000 per job, so each spare bunk is worth 10 tons of cargo space.

Is the Sparrow a bad choice?

Not bad — it's the fighter. It just carries little, so it's slower at the early money-making jobs.
