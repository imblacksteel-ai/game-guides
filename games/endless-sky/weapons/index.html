01

Conclusion

Every weapon sold in an outfitter at the start of the game — 100 weapons — with damage per second against shields and hull, range, energy and heat per second, ammo and where it's sold.

DPS is calculated from each weapon's reload time and per-shot damage (including submunitions), so you can compare guns of different fire rates directly. Remember the energy and heat columns: a weapon you can't power or cool won't reach its DPS.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Endless Sky's outfit and outfitter data. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Guns or turrets?

Guns fire forward; turrets aim themselves. Each ship has a limited number of gun ports and turret mounts, so compare DPS per outfit space in the table.

Why do some weapons list ammo?

Secondary weapons such as missiles need ammunition bought separately.

Why can't my ship use its weapons fully?

Check energy and heat: if your generators and coolers can't keep up, your weapons can't keep firing at full rate.
