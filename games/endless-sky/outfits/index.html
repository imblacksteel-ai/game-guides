Conclusion

Everything that isn't a weapon: engines, generators, batteries, shield generators and cooling sold at the start of the game, with their per-second output, energy and heat, and the outfit space they use.

The useful column is output per space: outfit space is the real budget on every ship, so a cheaper part that gives less per ton of space often loses to a pricier, denser one.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Endless Sky's outfit and outfitter data. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Why can't I fit a bigger engine?

Engines need engine capacity as well as outfit space; each ship has a fixed amount of each.

Do I need cooling?

If your heat generation outpaces your ship's heat dissipation, the ship overheats and shuts down. Compare the heat columns with your cooling.

Generators or batteries?

Generators produce energy continuously; batteries store a buffer for bursts like weapon fire.
