Licenses: What They Unlock

Which ships and outfits need a license, and which missions in the data grant each one.

01

Conclusion

Some ships and outfits can't be bought without a license. In the game's data, licenses are flags set by missions (“set license: …”), so the table below lists, for each license, which ships and outfits need it and which missions in the data grant it.

Most military licenses come through story campaigns, so if you want a particular warship, the table tells you which storyline to follow.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Endless Sky's ship, outfit and mission data. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Why does a license say “no way to get it found in the data”?

We searched every mission, event and shop in the current data and found nothing that grants it. That can mean the content is still being written, or that it's only for non-player factions (the Navy licenses, for example) — we couldn't confirm which.

How do I get the Pilot's License?

The default start gives it to you.

Can I buy a license?

Not with credits — licenses are granted by missions and story progress.

Why can't I see a licensed ship in the shipyard?

Ships that need a license you don't have can't be bought; get the license through the missions listed.
