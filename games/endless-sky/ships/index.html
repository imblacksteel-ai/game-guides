FAQ

Why is the price higher than the hull cost?

Ships are sold with their standard outfits; the price shown includes them.

What is free outfit space?

The outfit space left after the standard outfits — room for upgrades.

Can I buy alien ships?

Some, once you've reached their territory and they're willing to trade with you; story progress often matters.
