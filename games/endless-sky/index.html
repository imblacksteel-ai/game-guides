What Endless Sky Is

Endless Sky is a free, open-source space trading and combat game in the style of Escape Velocity: you start with a small ship and a mortgage, take jobs, trade, fight and upgrade your fleet across a large galaxy. The code and data are on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 license.

Because it's open source, the numbers on these pages come straight from its data files and code — including formulas the game doesn't show you, like exactly how jobs are paid.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Endless Sky's data files and source code. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Is Endless Sky free?

Yes. It's open source and free; it's also distributed on Steam at no cost.

Is there a Japanese version?

There's no official Japanese translation, so ship, outfit and system names stay in English on these pages.

Are these numbers current?

They're from the game's source at the commit of 2026-10-03, for the galaxy at the start of a new game.
