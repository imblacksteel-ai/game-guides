Game Overview

Civilization has sunk beneath the waves. Gather driftwood and salvage to expand your raft into a floating base. Progress splits into two tracks: base simulation and auto-battling crew combat.

Gather Resources

Collect debris floating around your raft, or risk shark-infested waters on a salvage boat and diving expeditions to pull wood, metal, and food from sunken wrecks.

Build Your Base

Construct and upgrade facilities like water purifiers and fishing spots. Assigning crew suited to each job boosts production efficiency.

Crew Combat

Line up roughly five crew members to fight automatically. Formation and composition decide the outcome in stages, world bosses, and PvP.

Reroll Tier List

Rerolling takes about 20 minutes. Here's the fastest route to unlocking the gacha, and which characters are worth holding out for.

How to Reroll

  1. 1Play through the tutorial until the Radio Station (gacha) unlocks.
  2. 2Claim every reward available from "Rebirth of the Water World" in the top right.
  3. 3Pull on the Radio Station.
  4. 4If you don't get who you want, delete and reinstall the app.
Already claimed the launch bonus? The full release handed out a free 210-pull login bonus, which is enough that some players skip rerolling entirely and just build around whoever they got. Weigh the ~20 minutes against what you already have before you start.

Best Characters

A power ranking based on investment priority. The axis is different from the reroll list above, so a few characters swap places.

SSMage

Scrap Vulture Oliver

Heals the whole team and boosts the crit rate of your highest-attack ally. An all-round support that stabilizes the party.

SSShooter

Steelman Cole

Teleports to the back line and fires an energy wave three columns wide at the densest cluster of enemies. Broad-range damage.

STank

Wide-area taunt, plus roughly one-third damage reduction with the rest reflected back as a counter. Top-tier tanking.

Summons two robo-dogs that keep chipping away at the front line. A core pick for teams that want more bodies on the field.

Early Game

If you're not sure what to do next, prioritize raising your Captain Level above everything else.

  • Prioritize resident requests Completing them grants Captain XP. Captain Level gates new content and each character's level cap. Use scheduled production at your facilities to keep fish, cloth, and water flowing efficiently.
  • Clear daily quests every day A reliable batch of Captain XP and items.
  • Push stages as far as you can win Idle rewards scale with how far you've progressed.
  • Gather resources by fishing, scavenging, and diving Your main sources of building and crafting materials — run them in parallel for efficiency.
  • Unlock the Relic Gacha and let it work passively Unlocks as Captain Level rises. Just owning a relic boosts your characters' stats.
Training tip: you don't need to level everyone. Use "lossless swap" when changing your lineup to carry levels over. If you're stuck, raise Captain Level before individual character levels.
Weekly bonus: clearing every daily quest across a full week can add up to roughly 200 SSR crew shards — a reliable, no-luck-involved way to push a specific crew member toward their next star rank.
