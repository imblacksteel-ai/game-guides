How to read this list

  1. 1Our tier is where the two Japanese guides agree. When they don't, it shows the range (for example S–SS).
  2. 2Battles are automatic, so a crew member's tier mostly reflects how much they do on their own: crowd control, tanking, buffs and summons rate highest.
  3. 3The four SS crew are also the standard reroll targets — any one of them is worth stopping for. The launch login bonus gave 210 free pulls, so many players skip rerolling entirely.
  4. 4A tier list is not a team: see the best team guide for how these crew fit together.
