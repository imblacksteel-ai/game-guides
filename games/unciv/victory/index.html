Victory Conditions

The five ways to win, with the exact milestones, projects and costs from Unciv's data.

01

Conclusion

Unciv's built-in ruleset has five ways to win: Scientific, Cultural, Domination, Diplomatic and Time. Each is a short list of milestones in the game's data, completed in order.

The heaviest single builds are the Utopia Project (1,500 production) for culture and the spaceship for science — the Apollo Program plus six parts at 750 production each.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's VictoryTypes, Buildings, Units and Speeds data files. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

05

FAQ

Can I turn victory types off?

Yes, when setting up a game. Projects like the Apollo Program and Utopia Project are only available when their victory is enabled.

Do I need to build the United Nations myself?

No — the milestone is that anyone builds it, then you need to win the vote.

What counts for Domination?

Capturing all the other capitals.
