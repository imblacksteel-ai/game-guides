UNCIV · VS CIV V

Unciv vs Civilization V

The differences the developers call intentional, plus what the code shows — for players coming from Civ V.

01

Conclusion

Unciv follows Civilization V: Gods & Kings closely, and most numbers are taken from Civ V references. But the developers list a few intentional departures, and the code shows some other differences — a fixed combat roll among them.

If you come from Civ V, the one that changes play the most is naval melee units attacking land units on the shore.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's own documentation of intentional departures from Civ V and its source code. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

Confirmed Differences

DifferenceWhat Unciv doesSource
Naval melee vs landWater melee units can attack land units on the shore — added because naval melee was considered too weak in Civ V.Unciv docs (intentional departures)
WorkersWorkers don't finish an improvement the turn they arrive; they receive orders and work on it, so you can change your mind.Unciv docs
VisibilityForests and jungles are visible one tile beyond your sight range, like hills — Civ V's behavior is treated as a bug.Unciv docs
Iroquois (Great Warpath)Forest and jungle tiles act as roads but don't give the railroad production bonus for connected cities.Unciv docs
Combat rollThe random part of combat damage is fixed by the turn and tile, so reloading doesn't change the outcome.BattleDamage.kt
03

The Developers' Stated Principles

  • “The AI plays to win.” The project's guiding principles say the AI should play like a human opponent — including attacking you if your military is weak.
  • Modding by combination. Uniques are designed to combine with conditions, so mods can mix effects freely — that's also why the game's text reads like building blocks (“+15% Strength <when attacking>”).
04

FAQ

Can I use Civ V guides for Unciv?

Mostly for strategy, but check numbers against Unciv's own data — this site's tables are read from it.

Is Brave New World content included?

The built-in rulesets are Civ V Vanilla and Gods & Kings. Other content comes from mods.

Why is the AI so aggressive?

It's by design: the developers want the AI to play to win, including attacking weak neighbors.
