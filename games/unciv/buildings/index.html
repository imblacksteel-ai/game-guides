Every Building: Cost, Upkeep and Effects

All 55 regular buildings, read straight from Unciv's data.

01

Conclusion

Unciv's built-in ruleset has 55 regular buildings (not counting wonders and civilization-unique replacements). Below, each is listed with its tech, the building it requires, production cost, gold upkeep and every effect.

Like wonders, most buildings take their cost from the tech column that unlocks them, so buildings from the same column cost the same. Watch the upkeep column: it's paid in gold every turn, so building everything everywhere isn't free.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's Buildings and Techs data files. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Why do some religious buildings have no tech or cost?

Buildings such as the Cathedral, Monastery, Mosque and Pagoda come from religion (bought with faith), so they don't sit in the tech tree.

Does difficulty change building cost?

Yes, for you: 50% on Settler, 67% Chieftain, 85% Warlord, 100% from Prince up. See Difficulty.

Where are the unique buildings?

Each civilization's unique buildings, and what they replace, are on the Civilizations page.
