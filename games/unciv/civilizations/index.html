All 34 Civilizations

Every ability and unique, read directly from Unciv's data — plus a stat comparison that shows why numbers alone don't tell the whole story.

01

Conclusion

The built-in ruleset has 34 civilizations. Each has one ability and usually two unique units or buildings that replace a standard one.

Reading the data side by side, one thing stands out: 24 of the 49 unique units have exactly the same strength and ranged strength as the unit they replace (a few differ in movement or cost). Their edge is in promotions and special abilities, which a plain stat table doesn't show — so don't judge a civilization by the numbers alone. The comparison table below lists those extra abilities next to each unit.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's Nations, Units and Buildings data files. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

04

Picking a Civilization (a Reading of the Data)

  • Bonuses that work with no setup are the easiest to feel as a new player: Egypt's +20% production on wonders, Babylon's free Great Scientist on Writing, Korea's +2 science per specialist, Rome's +25% production on buildings your capital already has.
  • Situational bonuses depend on the map or your plan: Spain needs natural wonders, Polynesia and Denmark shine with water, Celts need unimproved forest next to cities, Austria and Greece revolve around city-states.
  • The AI's preferences split 11 scientific, 9 cultural, 9 domination and 5 diplomatic — a rough map of what each civ was built for.
05

FAQ

Which civilization is the strongest?

The data can't settle that by itself — many unique units' strengths are in abilities, and map and difficulty matter a lot. Bonuses that need no setup are a sensible start.

Why do some unique units look identical to the normal unit?

Their advantage is a promotion or ability (for example extra combat bonuses), not base stats. Check the unit's details in the in-game Civilopedia.

Are these the same as Civ V?

They follow Civ V: Gods & Kings closely, but Unciv's data is the authority for Unciv.

Where do the Japanese ability texts come from?

The Japanese version of this page uses the game's own Japanese translation file.
