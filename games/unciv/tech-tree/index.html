Tech Tree: Every Tech and Unlock

All 80 technologies with their cost, prerequisites and unlocks, and the hidden factors in the cost formula.

01

Conclusion

Unciv's tech tree has 80 technologies in 18 columns, from Agriculture (base cost 20) to Future Tech (9,500). Below, every tech is listed with its base cost, prerequisites and everything it unlocks.

The code adds two things the tree doesn't show: each extra city raises tech costs by 5% on Tiny–Medium maps (3.75% Large, 2.5% Huge), and a tech gets cheaper when civilizations you know already have it — up to 30% cheaper when every remaining major civ has researched it.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's Techs data file and the tech cost formula in its source code. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

How Tech Cost Is Calculated

FactorEffect (from TechManager.kt)
DifficultyYour cost × the research modifier (90% on Settler, 95% Chieftain, 100% from Warlord up). Applies to human players.
Game speedQuick 67%, Standard 100%, Epic 150%, Marathon 300%.
Map sizeMedium ×1.1, Large ×1.2, Huge ×1.3.
Cities+5% per city beyond the first on Tiny–Medium, +3.75% Large, +2.5% Huge (puppets don't count).
Known civs who have itCost ÷ (1 + 0.3 × share of remaining major civs you know that have it).

FAQ

Why is my research slower than the tree suggests?

Map size and city count both raise tech cost. A Large map alone adds 20%, and each city adds 3.75% there.

Does meeting other civs help research?

Yes — techs that civs you know have already researched become cheaper for you.
