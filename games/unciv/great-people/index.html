Great People: Points and the Shared Pool

What each great person does, where points come from, and the shared threshold most players don't know about.

01

Conclusion

Reading GreatPersonManager.kt, the first great person needs 100 points (× game speed) and the requirement doubles each time. The catch: Great Scientists, Engineers, Merchants and Artists share one pool, so earning any of them doubles the cost of the next one of every kind. Great Prophets have their own pool.

Great Generals and Admirals come from combat instead: the first needs 200 points and each one after needs 50 more.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's Units, Specialists and Buildings data and GreatPersonManager.kt. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

Why did my Great Artist suddenly take longer?

Because you just got a Great Scientist, Engineer or Merchant — they share the same threshold.

How do I earn more points?

Work specialists (3 points each per turn) and build the buildings and wonders listed above.

Is the Great Prophet affected?

No, it has its own pool and threshold.
