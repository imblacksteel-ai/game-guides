Every Promotion and Effect

All 81 standard promotions, read straight from Unciv's data.

01

Conclusion

Units in Unciv's built-in ruleset can earn 81 standard promotions, split by unit type. Below is every one with its prerequisites, which unit types can take it, and its effect.

The data also holds unique-unit abilities as hidden promotions — that's where most unique units get their edge. Those are listed on the Civilizations page next to each unit.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's UnitPromotions data file. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

FAQ

How do units earn promotions?

From experience gained in combat; barracks-type buildings and some policies give starting experience.

Why can't my unit take a promotion?

It may need a prerequisite promotion, or the promotion may belong to a different unit type.

What does “Heal Instantly” do?

It heals 50 HP but uses up that promotion choice.
