Combat: Damage Formula and Calculator

The exact damage formula from Unciv's code, every combat modifier, and a calculator to check a fight before you take it.

01

Conclusion

Unciv's damage formula (BattleDamage.kt) depends on one number: the ratio of attack strength to defense strength after all bonuses. At equal strength each side deals 24–36 damage. The stronger side's damage grows quickly with the ratio, while the weaker side's shrinks.

Two things worth knowing: the random roll is fixed by the turn and tile, so reloading doesn't change the result; and a wounded unit deals less damage — 1% less for every 3 HP it's missing.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the combat code in Unciv's source (BattleDamage.kt and BattleConstants.kt). It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

04

Practical Takeaways

  • Stack defense bonuses. A unit fortified for 2 turns on a hill gets +65% defense — enough to turn an even fight into a lopsided one.
  • Attack with healthy units. A unit at 40 HP deals 20% less damage than at full health.
  • Surround before you strike. Each other melee unit next to the target adds 10% to every melee attack.
  • Soften with ranged units first. Ranged attackers take no retaliation (air units are the exception).
  • Don't attack over rivers or from boats if you can avoid it: −20% and −50%.
05

FAQ

Can I reload to get a better roll?

No. The roll comes from the turn number and the tile position, so the same attack on the same turn gives the same result.

Do civilians take damage?

A civilian defender always takes 40 damage (and is usually captured).

How do I see a unit's bonuses?

The in-game battle preview lists every modifier; add the ones that aren't covered here into the “other bonuses” boxes.
