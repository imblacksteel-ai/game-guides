Every Unit's Stats by Era

All 110 buildable units, read straight from Unciv's data files.

01

Conclusion

Below is every buildable unit in Unciv's built-in ruleset — 110 units, grouped by the era of the tech that unlocks them, with strength, ranged strength, range, movement, production cost, required resource and upgrade path.

Two things the table makes easy to see: which units need a strategic resource (horses, iron, oil, uranium and so on), and how each line upgrades into the next — useful for planning which early units are worth keeping.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of Unciv's Units and Techs data files. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

03

How to Read It

  • Strength is used for melee attack and defense; ranged is the ranged attack value.
  • Cost scales with difficulty for you (see Difficulty) and with game speed.
  • A unit that needs a resource can't be built without a spare copy of it.
  • Unit stats don't include promotions or special abilities — check the in-game Civilopedia for those.
04

FAQ

Why is a unit missing?

Barbarian-only units, great people and some special civilian units aren't in the table. Mods can also add units.

What does an upgrade cost?

Upgrade costs aren't stored as fixed numbers in the unit file, so they're not listed here; the game calculates them.

Are unique units included?

Yes, marked with their civilization. See Civilizations for how each compares with the unit it replaces.
