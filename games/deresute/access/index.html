Fixing Crashes and Lag

Deresute is known for crashing on some devices. Here's what causes it, what fixes it, and the one setup step to finish before you play.

Do this first: register a Namco ID

You need a Namco ID (Bandai Namco's account system) to play, and it's also how you carry your progress to a new device. Register right after installing — if your phone breaks or goes missing before you do, your progress can't be recovered.

Fixing crashes and stutter

  1. 1Switch live performances to 2D mode. It's far lighter than 3D and noticeably more stable on lower-end devices.
  2. 2Close other apps running in the background before launching — social media apps and browsers are the usual memory hogs.
  3. 3Restart your device before playing, especially if the game gets unstable after long sessions.
  4. 4If it still crashes, check your free storage. When space runs low, the game's cache writes are more likely to fail.
You don't lose anything in 2D: 3D lives look flashier, but the mode has no effect on your score or gameplay. Locking the game to 2D for stability is completely fine.
