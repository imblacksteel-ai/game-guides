KANCOLLE · REMODEL

How to Remodel (Kai, Kai Ni)

Remodeling is the biggest power jump most ships get. Here's how to do it, what it changes, and the five reasons the button sometimes won't work.

01

Conclusion

Remodeling (改造, Kaizō; the game also says 改装, Kaisō) is how a ship moves to its next form — for example 夕立 → 夕立改 → 夕立改二. It unlocks at a set level, costs ammo and steel, and takes effect instantly with no dock time.

Going by the wiki's rules, the things that trip people up are: you can't skip a stage, you need at least 4 free equipment slots, modernization bonuses reset (except luck, HP and ASW), and some remodels need items such as blueprints.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the remodel page on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.
02

How to Remodel

  1. 1From the home port, open Refit (改装, Kaisō).
  2. 2Pick the ship. If it has reached its remodel level, the Remodel (改造) button becomes available.
  3. 3Check the cost: ammo and steel, plus any items listed (development materials, Instant Construction, equipment, blueprints).
  4. 4Confirm. The change is immediate — the new form, stats and starting equipment appear right away.
03

What Changes When You Remodel

ThingWhat happens
StatsAlmost always go up. Right after remodeling, firepower, torpedo, AA and armor can vary a little, but they all reach a fixed higher value as the ship levels.
Modernization bonusesReset — except luck, HP and ASW, which carry over. Modernize after the remodel you plan to keep, not before.
Level and EXPCarried over. Remodeling later than the minimum level doesn't cost you stats.
HP, fuel, ammoFully restored. Fatigue is set to a fixed value, so sparkle is lost.
EquipmentYour old gear goes back to storage and the ship brings its own new starting gear — some of which you can't get any other way.
Slots and planesEquipment slots stay the same or increase. For carriers, the total plane count stays the same or rises, but individual slots can shrink.
Running costsFuel and ammo use usually rises. That's why some players keep expedition ships unremodeled.
Ship type and nameCan change (e.g. light cruiser → torpedo cruiser). Check expeditions and quests that need the old type first.
04

Why a Remodel Button Won't Work

  • Fewer than 4 free equipment slots. The game warns you and refuses. Discard some gear first.
  • A stage was skipped. Even at level 48, 那珂 (Naka) has to become 那珂改 before 那珂改二. There's no jumping ahead.
  • A required item is missing. Blueprints (改装設計図), Prototype Flight Deck Catapults (試製甲板カタパルト) and Action Reports (戦闘詳報) are common requirements for higher remodels.
  • The equipment it consumes is locked. If a remodel eats a piece of gear, unlock it in the equipment list first.
  • Not enough ammo or steel. Big ships cost more.
05

Getting Blueprints

A Blueprint (改装設計図, Kaisō Sekkeizu) is usually made by trading 4 medals (勲章, Kunshō) from the item screen. Medals come from clearing the end-of-world maps each month (up to 7 a month) and from some quests. A few quests and events give blueprints directly.

The wiki counts 103 blueprints needed across every ship that uses them, so medals are a long-term budget. Pick which remodels to spend them on — see Which Ships to Remodel First.

06

FAQ

Should I remodel the moment a ship hits the level?

Usually yes for combat ships. One tip from the wiki: if a ship at its remodel level is badly damaged, remodeling restores its HP for free instead of paying for repairs.

Can I undo a remodel?

Normal remodels are one-way. Some ships have convert remodels (コンバート改装) that let you switch between two forms, at a resource cost each time.

Why did my carrier lose planes?

The remodel changed the slot sizes. The total is the same or higher, but planes in a shrunken slot can't all be refilled.

Do I need to modernize again after remodeling?

Yes, except for luck, HP and ASW. That's why it's usually better to modernize after the final remodel you plan to use.
