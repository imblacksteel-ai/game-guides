Overview

KanColle popularized the "girls representing warships" genre years before it had any real competition. It's still Japanese-only over a decade later, but a large unofficial English-speaking community has kept it alive the whole time — this page exists because that demand never went away.

Collect Kanmusu

Recruit shipgirls (kanmusu) representing real WWII-era vessels, from destroyers up to battleships, and grow your fleet.

Manage a Fleet

Balance four resources — fuel, ammo, steel, and bauxite — across sorties, expeditions, and repairs.

Fight in Waves

Sortie through map nodes against the Abyssal Fleet, resolve air and shelling phases, and clear boss nodes to complete an area.

Getting Started

The menus are all in Japanese, but the early flow is simple once you know the shape of it.

  1. 1Pick your Admiral name. It can't be changed later — don't overthink it, but don't be careless either.
  2. 2Choose a starter ship. The four choices are close enough in stats; pick whichever destroyer you like the look of.
  3. 3Check your Quests (任務) at the top of the screen — they reward resources and unlock features. Do the ones you can.
  4. 4Complete the 6-ship fleet organization quest. This is your first real milestone and unlocks your second fleet slot.
  5. 5Farm early nodes like map 1-1 for destroyers — it's the standard early spot to build up your roster.
  6. 6Unlock Expeditions (遠征) by clearing the 2nd fleet quest, so you can gather resources in parallel with sorties.
  7. 7Don't rush every daily quest or ranking board. You don't need to clear everything from day one — pace yourself.
Which ships to keep: prioritize ships with a strong remodel (Kai) line and low fuel/ammo upkeep — cheap ships pay for themselves on expeditions. It's fine to scrap extra copies of common early ships once you already have one you like.
Before an event: event maps come in multiple difficulties (丙/乙/甲, easiest to hardest) with better rewards at higher difficulty. Build up a land-based air squadron (基地航空隊) ahead of time — aim for at least 4 planes per squadron, ideally 8–9 — and consider developing a Type 2 Ka reconnaissance seaplane for the scouting bonus it grants.

Equipment Development

The Arsenal (工廠) doubles as a blueprint lab: feed it fuel, ammo, steel, and bauxite in the right ratios and it favors specific gear instead of a random result. These are the community-standard recipes.

Not a guarantee: a recipe raises your odds of pulling from that gear pool, it doesn't lock the result. Development also consumes a development materials (開発資材) item alongside the four resources above.
