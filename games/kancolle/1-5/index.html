1-5 Guide: Fleet and Anti-Submarine Tips

1-5 is where you learn to fight submarines. Here's the 4-ship fleet that locks the boss route, how ASW works, and one trap to avoid.

01

Conclusion

1-5 (鎮守府近海) is the first Extra Operation map and the first place you fight submarines. Sink the boss flagship 4 times each month to clear it for a medal and 75 special war results.

From the wiki's routing table, the boss route is easy to lock: 4 ships or fewer, no (non-aviation) battleships, standard carriers or oilers, at most 2 light cruisers and 1 light carrier. A light cruiser and three destroyers in line abreast, carrying sonar and depth charges, is the standard approach.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the 1-5 routing table and notes on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.
02

Fleet and Routing

RuleEffect

No battleships (aviation battleships are OK), standard carriers or submarinesAny of these sends you away from the boss.
At most 2 light cruisers and 1 light carrier3+ light cruisers or 2+ light carriers branch away.
No oilersOilers make some branches random. Yamashiomaru counts as an oiler.

The wiki lists 1 light cruiser + 3 destroyers, 4 destroyers, 4 destroyer escorts — even 4 aviation battleships — as fleets that reach the boss by the shortest route.

Use exactly 4 ships. With 3 or fewer, you can't choose a formation and are forced into line ahead, which is the worst formation against submarines.
03

Fighting Submarines

  • Equipment matters more than base stats for ASW. Sonar and depth charges are the core; develop them if you don't have them.
  • Line abreast (単横陣) gives the best damage against submarines.
  • Finish in the day battle. At night, attacks on submarines only do scratch damage.
  • Opening ASW (先制対潜) lets you hit subs before their opening torpedoes. It normally needs 100 ASW including gear plus a sonar; destroyer escorts need only 60. Isuzu Kai Ni (level 50) can do it with no conditions, which makes her the usual answer for new players.
  • Anti-submarine nodes on the way (A, D, F) use only 8% fuel and no ammo. The boss node uses the normal 20%/20%.
04

Watch Your HQ Level

1-5 is cheap and gives a lot of admiral EXP, so farming the boss raises your HQ level quickly — to around 80 faster than you'd expect. Some enemy fleets get stronger with HQ level, including on 1-5 itself: around HQ 60, more submarines with opening torpedoes appear.

For leveling destroyers and light cruisers, the wiki suggests going with 6 ships and turning back before the boss instead of farming the boss with 4.

06

FAQ

Can Akashi drop here?

Yes, at the boss with an S rank, but only from a boss fleet that appears at higher HQ levels and at a low rate. See How to Get Akashi.

A submarine keeps heavily damaging my ships before I can act.

That's an opening torpedo. Opening ASW (or Isuzu Kai Ni) is the fix; line abreast and better sonar help too.

When does the gauge come back?

At the start of each month. Clears count for the medal until 23:59 JST and for special war results until about 22:00 on the last day.

Does damage to the boss reduce the gauge?

No — only sinking the boss flagship does, at 25% each time.
