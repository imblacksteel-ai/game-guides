KANCOLLE · SHIP CAP

Dock Full? Expanding Space and What to Scrap

You've hit the ship cap and the game won't let you build. Here's how to buy more room, how to make room without regretting it, and why the lock button should come first.

01

Conclusion

When you hit your ship cap you have two options: make room or buy more room. More room means the paid Port Expansion (母港拡張, Bokō Kakuchō): +10 ship slots and +40 equipment slots per purchase.

For making room, the way I read it is: feed spare duplicates into Modernization (近代化改修) instead of scrapping them, and lock every ship you want to keep first so you can't throw it away by mistake.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the item, lock and modernization rules on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.
02

Lock Your Ships First

KanColle saves every action instantly, so there's no undo for a scrapped ship. The lock (ロック, rokku) feature exists for exactly this:

  • A locked ship can't be used as Modernization material — it doesn't even appear in the list.
  • A locked ship can't be scrapped (解体, Kaitai) — it shows up, but the confirm button is disabled.
  • Nothing else changes: locked ships sortie, get modernized and level up as normal.
  • To lock one, open Organize (編成), press Change (変更) on any slot to open the ship list, and tap the lock icon on the ship.

04

Modernize Instead of Scrapping

Scrapping (解体) a ship returns a few resources. Feeding it into Modernization (近代化改修, Kindaika Kaishū) from the Refit (改装) menu frees the same slot and raises stats such as firepower, torpedo, anti-air or armor on the ship you keep. If you're going to remove a ship anyway, modernization is usually the better use of it.

Equipment fills up too: each Port Expansion also adds 40 equipment slots. Discarding (廃棄, Haiki) duplicate low-tier guns and planes frees equipment space — but check quests first, because some conversion quests consume specific equipment.
05

FAQ

Is Port Expansion the only way to raise the cap?

It's the standard way, and it's a paid item (1,000 points in the item shop).

I scrapped a ship by accident. Can I get it back?

No — actions are saved immediately and there's no undo. You'd have to find or build another copy. That's why locking comes first.

Should I scrap duplicates during an event?

Usually no. With event tags, an untagged duplicate can sortie into a map the tagged one is locked out of. See Event Ship Locks.
