Fleet Speed is set by your slowest ship. A single Low-speed ship drags the whole fleet down to Low — many expeditions and event maps require High speed or better, so check this before you commit fuel. Your fleet's AA total is what you'd plug into the Air Superiority Calculator as one slot's worth of input (sum every ship's AA slots there for the real per-aircraft-slot formula — this page just totals the ship stat).
