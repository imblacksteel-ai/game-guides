03

Large Ship Construction

Large Ship Construction (大型艦建造) unlocks through a quest once you're running a third fleet. It's the only way to build Yamato, Musashi, Taihou and most foreign ships, but it's expensive and very random.

  • Each resource takes 1,500 / 1,500 / 2,000 / 1,000 at minimum (fuel / ammo / steel / bauxite) and up to 7,000. You also choose 1, 20 or 100 Development Materials; more is officially said to help with rarer ships.
  • Skipping the timer costs 10 Instant Construction.
  • Leaving your other docks empty is officially said to help a little, but players haven't been able to measure any difference.
  • Foreign ships need the secretary shown in the build-time table above — for example Bismarck only appears with Z1 or Z3 as secretary.

04

How construction works

  • Each resource can be set from 30 to 999, in the order Fuel / Ammo / Steel / Bauxite. Every build also uses one Development Material.
  • Construction never fails, and your HQ level doesn't change what can appear.
  • The secretary ship doesn't matter — except for the foreign ships marked in the table, which only appear with the listed secretary.
  • Rare destroyers (Shimakaze, Yukikaze, Z1, Z3) and rare light cruisers (Ooi, Kinu, Abukuma) never come out of the 30/30/30/30 recipe.
  • Per the developers, a large-gun ship needs a minimum amount of steel and a balance that avoids the carrier table (bauxite matters most there); carriers need extra bauxite and fuel on top of enough steel.
  • An Instant Construction (flamethrower) skips the timer. Large Ship Construction is a separate system; the ships only available from it are listed in the last column above.
