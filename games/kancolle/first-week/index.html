Your First Week as a New Admiral

The first week sets up everything after it. Here's a sample plan, how quests work, and the early mistakes the wiki warns about.

01

Conclusion

The Japanese wiki's beginner guide boils the first days down to a simple loop: accept quests, do them, claim them, while growing a fleet of destroyers and light cruisers and getting expeditions running.

The way I read it, the common first-week mistake is spending resources chasing battleships. Big ships cost a lot to run, construction is random, and most early quests and expeditions want small ships anyway.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the beginner guide on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

03

Which Starter Should I Pick?

According to the wiki, the five starters are almost identical at the start, and the others are easy to get later — so pick the one you like.

If you want the long-term edge: Fubuki (吹雪) has the most remodel stages and brings rare gear, and Murakumo (叢雲) also has a Kai Ni. Inazuma, Fubuki and Murakumo are named in some later quests.

04

How Quests Work

  • Open Quests (任務) and click a quest to set it in progress (遂行中). You can have up to 5 active at the start.
  • When it shows complete (達成), click it again to claim the reward.
  • The tutorial doesn't lock anything — but if its instructions are confusing, just clear the quests named “はじめての○○” (“first ___”).
  • Daily quests reset at 5:00 JST, weekly ones on Monday 5:00, monthly ones on the 1st. If you run out of quests, the wiki's advice is simply to rest until the next reset.

Things to Know Early

Battleships earlyUsually hard to sustain: big resupply and repair costs, and construction may give a destroyer instead. Most important ships also drop from maps.
Expeditions failingSuccess depends on flagship level, required ship types, number of ships, and full fuel/ammo. Damage doesn't matter, but a heavily damaged flagship can't go.
Resource regenStops above a cap set by HQ level. Expeditions and quests aren't capped.
06

FAQ

Is Yamato the strongest? Should I aim for her?

She's powerful, but the wiki points out you can't build or run her early on. Ship types have different strengths, and rarity says little about strength.

Should I use Instant Construction?

You get it steadily from quests, but it's also needed for strong friend fleets in events and in large amounts for some remodels. The wiki's advice is to save it unless you're in a hurry. Using it doesn't change which ship you get — that's decided when construction starts.

How do I get more fleets?

Through organization quests. See Unlock More Fleets.

I'm out of resources.

Keep expeditions running and come back later. See Running Out of Resources.
