KANCOLLE · REMODEL PRIORITY

Which Ships to Remodel First

EXP and blueprints are limited. Here's a level-cost-first way to decide which remodels to chase, with the early Kai Ni that need no blueprint.

01

Conclusion

The way I read the remodel tables, the order is simple: give every ship you use its first remodel (Kai) as soon as you can — it's cheap and the jump is big — then put your EXP into Kai Ni remodels that unlock at a low level and need no blueprint.

Save blueprints (4 medals each) for remodels that change how a ship plays, and don't spend them early on ships you may not use.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the remodel level table on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

04

How I'd Think About Priority

  • Level cost first. Going from 50 to 75 takes far more EXP than going from 20 to 50, so a Kai Ni at 48–60 pays off much sooner than one at 75+. See the EXP calculator.
  • Role second. A strong ASW ship or torpedo cruiser fills a gap your fleet probably has early on; a fourth similar destroyer doesn't.
  • Blueprints last. Medals come slowly (up to 7 a month), and the wiki counts 103 blueprints needed across all ships. Spend them on ships you know you'll use for a long time.
  • Expedition ships are the exception. Remodeling raises fuel and ammo use, and some ships change type. It's fine to keep a few destroyers and light cruisers unremodeled for expeditions.
Careful with type changes: Kitakami and Ooi become torpedo cruisers at Kai (Lv10), and Kiso at Kai Ni. Some expeditions and quests ask for light cruisers, so keep that in mind before remodeling.
05

FAQ

Is it ever bad to remodel?

Mostly only for expedition ships (higher costs) or when a quest or expedition needs the old ship type. For combat ships, remodeling is almost always the upgrade.

Which blueprint remodel should I do first?

It depends on your fleet, and there's no single right answer. A common approach is to pick a ship you'll use in many maps rather than a niche one.

Should I level a ship past its remodel level before remodeling?

There's no stat penalty either way — stats scale with level. Remodel when it's convenient (or when the ship is damaged, to get a free repair).

Where do I see a ship's remodel level?

In Refit (改装), select the ship; the remodel information shows the required level and items.
