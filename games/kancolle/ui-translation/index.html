KANCOLLE · UI TRANSLATION

UI Translation: Every Menu Button Explained

KanColle is Japanese only, but you only need about a dozen words to play. Here's every button you'll actually press, with romaji and what it does — and the one layout quirk you really need to watch.

01

Conclusion

You can play KanColle without reading Japanese if you memorize about a dozen words. The six home-port buttons (出撃・編成・補給・改装・入渠・工廠) cover almost everything you do day to day.

The one that actually costs people ships: after a battle, Retreat is on the right (進撃 left / 撤退 right), but at the night-battle prompt, “Don't pursue” is on the left (追撃せず left / 夜戦突入 right). The safe choice switches sides. Read the button, don't click from habit.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the menu labels and beginner FAQ on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

Battle Screens

The two decision prompts

PromptLeft buttonRight buttonSafe choice
After a daytime battle進撃 (Shingeki) — Advance撤退 (Tettai) — RetreatRight (Retreat)
Before night battle追撃せず (Tsuigeki sezu) — Don't pursue夜戦突入 (Yasen Totsunyū) — Enter night battleLeft (Don't pursue)

The developer said in a 2013 interview that this reversed layout is one of the most common complaints from new admirals — it was meant to separate “special” from “basic” actions. The wiki's tip: left to keep going forward, right to go home at the advance prompt.

Common Mistakes

  1. 1Clicking the same side at both prompts. Retreat is right, “don't pursue” is left. This is the single biggest cause of accidental sinkings.
  2. 2Docking a ship that's carrying gear you need elsewhere. Docked ships can't change equipment — move items off before you repair, or borrow them with “他艦娘装備中”.
  3. 3Mixing up 解体 and 廃棄. 解体 scraps a ship; 廃棄 throws away equipment.
  4. 4Doing a task before accepting its quest. Quests (任務) only count progress while they're accepted.
09

FAQ

Is there an official English version?

No — KanColle's interface is Japanese only. Learning the labels on this page is the practical way in.

What happens if my flagship is heavily damaged?

Unless the flagship carries Damage Control, the game shows 「進撃が困難です」 (“advancing is difficult”) and sends the fleet home automatically. A fleet whose flagship is heavily damaged can't sortie, exercise or go on expeditions until you repair or swap it.

Why does a formation choice sometimes not appear?

Formations only unlock once the fleet has four or more ships.
