KANCOLLE · DESTROYER ESCORTS

How to Get Destroyer Escorts

Destroyer escorts are cheap, anti-submarine specialists. Here's where the wiki's drop tables list them, other ways to get them, and what they're actually good for.

01

Conclusion

Destroyer escorts (海防艦, Kaibōkan, DE) mostly come from map drops, events and limited-time quests. In the wiki's normal-map drop tables, the most accessible source is the 1-5 boss, which lists Kaibokan No.4 and No.22. World 7's 7-4 has the widest selection.

They're worth having: the wiki notes they can do opening anti-submarine attacks at only 60 ASW (other ships need 100), and they cost about as little to run as submarines.

An analysis-based guide: this guide is based on our analysis of the drop tables and ship type notes on the Japanese KanColle wiki. It describes what the data shows and is not a guarantee of results.

03

Other Ways to Get Them

  • Events. Destroyer escorts often appear as event drops or clear rewards.
  • Limited-time quests. For example, Etorofu has been a choose-one reward in several seasonal quests alongside Matsuwa, Sado, Tsushima and Fukae, according to her wiki page.
  • Keep a free ship slot and at least 4 free equipment slots, or nothing can drop. See Dock Full?

05

FAQ

Can I build destroyer escorts?

They don't appear in the wiki's normal construction recipe table, so plan around drops, events and quests.

Are they good for 1-5?

Yes — the wiki lists 4 destroyer escorts as a fleet that reaches the 1-5 boss by the shortest route, and their low opening-ASW threshold suits it.

Do they count as destroyers for routing?

Many routing rules count them together with destroyers (駆逐+海防), but not all. Check the specific map. See Routing Explained.

Should I bring them to events?

For anti-submarine nodes and some expeditions, yes. Against surface fleets, their low durability makes them risky.
