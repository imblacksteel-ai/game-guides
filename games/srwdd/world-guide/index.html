SRW DD · WORLD GUIDE

Which World Should You Start With?

Your run starts inside one of 6 "Worlds," each themed around a different slice of the crossover cast. Here's what actually differs between them — and why it's a lower-stakes choice than it looks.

The short answer: two independent community sources confirm that Worlds you don't start in can still be played and cleared later — starting World isn't a permanent lock-out. Pick based on which franchise you want to see first, not fear of missing out.

The 6 Worlds

Each World is styled after a different era/sub-series of the mainline Super Robot Wars franchise, and headlines a different slice of the crossover cast. Rosters below are the franchises most commonly cited for each World — treat them as representative, not a complete list.

How to actually decide

Since starting World does determine which units you get access to first — this isn't purely cosmetic — two approaches are common:

  1. 1Favorite-franchise pick: just start wherever your favorite series is. Every source agrees any unit clears the game fine if you invest in it, so there's no wrong answer here.
  2. 2Efficiency pick: if you rerolled for a specific SSR special-attack part (see our Reroll Guide), start in whichever World contains a mobile suit that part can equip to, for a stronger opening few missions.
