SRW DD · REROLL

Reroll Guide

Rerolling in SRW DD is unusually fast — about 2 minutes per attempt — because the gacha drops equippable parts, not full units. Here's exactly what you're looking for and why.

Why the gacha isn't what you'd expect

If you've played other mecha gacha games, the instinct is to reroll for a specific unit. SRW DD's gacha instead drops unit parts — items that grant a special attack when equipped to a compatible mobile suit. A "good pull" isn't a rare unit, it's an SSR part carrying a strong special attack, since that part can go on any unit you already own or unlock later.

How to reroll

  1. 1Play through the tutorial until you reach the gacha screen — this is most of the ~2 minutes.
  2. 2Pull with whatever starting currency and tickets the game hands you.
  3. 3Check the result against the target list below.
  4. 4Good pull: continue and pick your starting World. Not good: delete the app, reinstall, repeat.

What to hold out for

SSRNightingale (MAP attack) SSRHi-ν Gundam (MAP attack)

Both grant a MAP attack — a special move that hits an area instead of one target — and both units have high Speed, so their turn comes around often. An SSR part carrying either of these moves is considered the strongest realistic reroll outcome; you're not likely to do meaningfully better by rerolling further once you have one.

What counts as "good enough"? Any SSR part with a special attack is a reasonable stopping point if you don't want to spend more time rerolling — SR-and-below results are the ones worth retrying for. The two MAP-attack SSRs above are the ceiling, not the floor.
