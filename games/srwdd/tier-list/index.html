SRW DD · TIER LIST

Best Parts to Build Around

SRW DD doesn't really have "unit" tier lists — it has special-attack part tier lists. Here's what the Japanese community consistently ranks at the top, and which of those you can actually get without relying on gacha luck.

Why parts, not units

The gacha drops unit parts, not finished units — a part grants a special attack when equipped on a compatible mobile suit. So "best unit" really means "best chassis carrying the best part." A rare mobile suit with a weak part underperforms a common one carrying a top-tier part.

General rule: parts that help your whole team just by being deployed — area buffs, debuffs, MAP attacks — are consistently valued over parts that only hit a single enemy hard, no matter how big that hit is. When choosing between two SSR parts, lean toward the one with a team-wide effect.
