Taiketsusen (Duel Battle) Explained

Taiketsusen (対決戦) is SRW DD's PVP ranking event — auto-battled 8-vs-8 matches against other players' teams. Here's how it actually works, which units keep showing up in strong teams, and how to climb without spending.

Not to be confused with Geigekisen (迎撃戦): that's a separate score-based ranking event — 5 areas, 4 units deployed per area, no reusing a unit across areas (20 units needed total), competing against a leaderboard rather than other players directly. Taiketsusen is the actual PVP mode: real 8v8 matches against other players' squads.

How Taiketsusen works

  • Unlock: clear EXTRA Stage 5 in any World.
  • Team size: up to 8 units per side.
  • Battles: auto-battled — you build your team, then the match resolves automatically rather than being manually controlled turn by turn.
  • Attempts: costs PP. You get up to 7 PP per day from natural regeneration (full refill daily during the event). PP Recovery items refill it too, and D-Crystals can refill PP at a rate of 5 D-Crystals per PP recovery, usable up to twice a day — so a fully-resourced day caps out around 11 attempts.
  • Rewards: winning raises your rating, and your rank at the end of the event determines rewards — including unit-upgrade materials and general-purpose skill orbs.
Don't skip it when it's running: Taiketsusen is the only source of "super-modification materials" (超改造素材) — and it's a limited-time event held irregularly, not a permanent mode. Participate every time it's available, even just for the daily PP attempts, since you can't get this material any other way.

PVP-focused community analysis (particularly a series of Taiketsusen write-ups on note.com) repeatedly names these as near-essential or consistently strong picks. Treat this as commonly-cited, not a definitive ranking — the PVP metagame shifts with each Taiketsusen run as new units release.

Nightingale — already a top pick for PVE in our Tier List — is also called out as an emerging strong PVP pick, described as an upgrade over the previous generation of Speed-625 attackers. If you already have it for PVE, it carries over to Taiketsusen too.

Reaching S-rank without spending on support items

A community guide focused on reaching S-rank without buying Awakening/Courage support items describes substituting them with auto-triggering spirit commands from skill orbs: certain pilots auto-trigger "Courage" once morale hits 130+, and pairing an auto-triggering "Hot Blood" with "Sure Hit" is reported to approximate the effect of the paid support items. Since auto-battle means you can't manually time these, getting them to trigger passively via equipped skills is the practical workaround for a F2P/low-spend team.
