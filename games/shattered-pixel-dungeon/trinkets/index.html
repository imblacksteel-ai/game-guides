Trinkets

Trinkets change how a run plays rather than making you stronger directly — some trade a benefit for a drawback. Here's exactly what each one does at every level.

  • Trinkets are made at an alchemy pot from a magical catalyst and upgraded there with alchemical energy, up to +3.
  • Several trinkets cut both ways: the Dimensional Sundial adds enemies at night, the Eye of Newt shrinks your vision, the Salt Cube slows healing and the Ferret Tuft also makes enemies harder to hit.
  • The Parchment Scrap is worth taking all the way: weapon and armor curses become more common at +0 and +1 but disappear entirely at +3.
