01

Overview

Shattered Pixel Dungeon is developed by Evan Debenham (Shattered Pixel), based on the original Pixel Dungeon, and its full source code is public under the GPL-3.0 license. Each run is a fresh, randomly generated descent: you pick a hero, find and identify items as you go, and push down through the dungeon's regions and bosses. Death is permanent.

03

How Strength Affects Weapons

Every weapon has a strength (STR) requirement. Tier 1 needs 10, and each tier adds 2 (a few heavy weapons need more).

  1. 1Too weak: for each point of STR you're missing, accuracy is divided by 1.5 and each attack takes 1.2× as long — and both stack, so even 2 points short hurts a lot.
  2. 2Extra strength: every hit gets a random bonus of 0 up to your excess STR (so 3 extra STR adds 0–3 damage per hit).
  3. 3Upgrading lowers the requirement: the STR requirement drops by 1 at +1, +3, +6, +10 and so on.

Reading the numbers

Wand damage, charges and every ring's bonus →

Every trinket's effect at +0 to +3 →

  • The highest raw damage per turn in each tier: Studded Gloves (tier 1), Sickle (tier 2, but only ×0.68 accuracy), Sai (tier 3), Flail (tier 4, ×0.8 accuracy) and Greataxe (tier 5, which needs 20 STR).
  • Fast weapons like Gloves, Sai and Gauntlet split their damage over two hits per turn, so enemy armor, which reduces every hit, cuts into them more than into slow, heavy weapons.
  • Several weapons have effects the table can't show — the Dagger and Assassin's Blade are built for surprise attacks, and the Runic Blade gains more damage per upgrade than other tier-4 weapons. Accuracy-boosted weapons like the Cudgel, Hand Axe and Mace trade damage for fewer misses.
