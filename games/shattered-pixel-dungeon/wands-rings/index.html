Wands & Rings

Which wand hits hardest, how fast charges come back, and what each ring actually gives at each upgrade level. Numbers are from the game's source code.

01

Wands

Damage per zap at +0 and what each upgrade adds (min / max). Utility wands don't deal direct damage. From v4.0.1.

  • Every wand starts with 2 charges (Magic Missile has 3), gains +1 per upgrade, and caps at 10.
  • Charges come back one at a time, and faster the emptier the wand is: each charge takes 10 + 40 × 0.875missing charges turns — about 45 turns with 1 charge missing, 36.8 with 3 missing and 20.5 with 10 missing. A Ring of Energy speeds this up.
  • Damage and effects scale with the wand's level, so upgrades on a wand you use a lot pay off twice: more damage per zap and more charges.

Rings

Rings give their bonus in steps. A ring's step count is its level + 1, so even a +0 ring gives one step, and each upgrade adds another. Wearing two of the same ring adds their steps together.
