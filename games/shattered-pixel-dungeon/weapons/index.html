Conclusion

Every melee weapon in Shattered Pixel Dungeon v4.0.1 with its tier, strength requirement, damage, damage per upgrade and average damage per turn — read from each weapon's class in the game's source code.

The table can't show everything, so the section below lists the special rules for the weapons people look up most: the Runic Blade, Crossbow, Assassin's Blade, Glaive, Greataxe, Katana, Sai, War Scythe and Sickle.

Special Rules for Popular Weapons

WeaponWhat the code adds beyond the table
Runic BladeTier 4 with a tier-3 base max (20) but +6 max damage per upgrade (normal tier 4 is +5), so it catches up at +5 and pulls ahead after. The Duelist's ability boosts its enchantment to 300% (+50% per upgrade) for one strike.
CrossbowWhile equipped, your thrown darts deal 4–12 damage (+1 / +3 per crossbow upgrade) instead of a dart's normal 1–2. Its own melee max is 20, +4 per upgrade.
Assassin's BladeOn a surprise attack, damage rolls between halfway-to-max and max instead of min to max.
GlaiveReach 2 and 0.67× attack speed, with a 40 base max and +8 per upgrade.
Greataxe45 base max damage but needs 20 strength (two more than a normal tier 5).
KatanaAdds 3 armor (blocking) while wielded.
SaiAttacks twice per turn (0.5 turns per attack) with a low 10 max, +2 per upgrade.
War Scythe40 base max damage at tier 5, but 20% less accuracy.
Sickle20 base max at tier 2 (well above its tier), but 32% less accuracy.
04

FAQ

Is the Runic Blade worth it?

It starts weaker than other tier-4 weapons but gains more per upgrade; it's equal at +5 and better above. It's a weapon for runs where you plan to pour upgrades into it.

Does the Crossbow need special darts?

No — while it's equipped, all your darts deal 4–12 damage (plus its upgrades).

Which weapon has the most damage per turn?

Sort the table by average per turn, but remember accuracy: the War Scythe and Sickle hit less often.
